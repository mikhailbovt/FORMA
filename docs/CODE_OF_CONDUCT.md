# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Forma's community is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive environment
for contributors and users, regardless of background, identity, experience,
ability, or circumstance. Technical disagreement is expected; harassment and
dehumanization are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Make room for people with different experience levels and communication
  styles.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic resume data and redact personal information,
  credentials, cookies, prompts, and private correspondence.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help move the work forward.
- Follow maintainer guidance when a discussion needs to pause or change venue.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language, slurs, sexualized attention, or unwelcome advances.
- Personal insults, doxxing, outing, or publishing private information without
  explicit permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, personal resume data, or security details.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.
- Other conduct a reasonable person would consider hostile or unsafe in a
  professional open-source community.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community
spaces, and public interactions where someone represents Forma or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the repository owner
or maintainers through a private channel listed on their GitHub profiles. For a
report involving a maintainer, contact a different maintainer when possible.
Include links, dates, context, and the outcome you need, but avoid distributing
sensitive information more widely than necessary.

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical,
give affected people a chance to be heard, and act in proportion to context,
severity, and prior behavior. Responses may include a private correction,
content removal, a warning, a temporary participation restriction, or a
permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow this policy may also be removed
from project responsibilities.

Good-faith reports will not be punished. Knowingly false reports or weaponized
complaints may themselves violate this policy.

## Questions

Questions about this policy may be raised privately with maintainers or, when no
personal details are involved, in a public project discussion.
